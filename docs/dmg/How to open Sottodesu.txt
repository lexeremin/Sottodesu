Sottodesu - how to install
==========================

1. Drag "Sottodesu" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
2. Open Sottodesu from your Applications folder (or Launchpad).

If macOS says Sottodesu "cannot be opened" or "could not be verified":
   a. Click "Done" (do not move it to the Trash).
   b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   c. Scroll down to the Security section and click "Open Anyway" next to Sottodesu.
   d. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open".
You only have to do this once.

Why does this happen? Sottodesu is free and open source and is not yet registered with Apple.
It never connects to the internet and only touches your Downloads folder.

Help and the source code: https://github.com/lexeremin/Sottodesu
